# Original media and evidence

Use the named WhatsApp conversation through its signed-in UI. Never export unrelated conversation history or read private app databases/session cookies as a shortcut.

Record exact captions, directly relevant replies and timestamps with timezone when known. Do not invent missing IDs or dates. Open/download original media, then verify the selected viewer caption before saving privately. Screenshots of blurred placeholders are not sources.

If screenshots and accessibility disagree, re-observe and prefer supported accessible controls. Check focus before typing; clear accidental unsent drafts. Keyboard navigation in the media chooser can reach adjacent photos when pointer controls fail. Inspect the chosen caption again before downloading.

Describe the intended product and explicitly exclude overlapping fixtures. Multiple equally plausible subjects require clarification. Group multiple views under one product. File hashes deduplicate exact files; visually compare different files before treating them as another item.

Associate prices from explicit captions, replies or unambiguous context. Retain literal evidence alongside amount and currency. Conflicting prices stay unresolved. User-confirmed selling prices do not justify inferring discounts from old tags. Tax inclusion is separate; preserve relevant qualifiers or resolve their association before public price use. Unknown stock is neither zero nor proof of availability.

If original-media access remains unavailable after supported retries, request the exact missing attachments and continue independent reusable work. Do not substitute existing catalog products.
