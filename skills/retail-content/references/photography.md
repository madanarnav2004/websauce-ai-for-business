# Photographic direction and fidelity

Include this verbatim in every prompt and revision:

> Do not change the dimensions or proportions of the product. Preserve its shape, component count, construction, colour, finish, pattern and visible details. Remove unrelated retail clutter. Produce realistic commercial photography with natural materials, believable lighting and contact shadows. Do not invent hidden features or make the product look generically AI-generated.

Add source-grounded subject boundaries, excluded fixtures, visible construction and the camera angle that can be retained. Never infer physical measurements from perspective.

- **Catalog:** neutral seamless or restrained architectural backdrop; complete product, comfortable margins, controlled highlights and truthful colour. No rendered labels or prices.
- **Lifestyle:** plausible setting, restrained furnishings and believable scale/attachment. Retain an evidenced camera angle. Label it a visualization, not a customer installation.
- **Detail:** a visible feature at source-supported magnification. Do not fabricate texture from low-resolution ambiguity; use a distinct second setting when detail is insufficient.
- **Fourth:** add only if it answers a different buyer question. Tiny crop and tint variations do not count as distinct concepts.

Default feed framing is 4:5; reels are 9:16. Check the current destination composer at release time. Never stretch the product to fill a ratio. Use built-in image generation for photographic edits; deterministic text/canvas processing must follow the active image tool rules.

Prompt order: source roles → subject/distractors → scene → framing → light/materials → invariant paragraph → exclusions. Reference originals on every revision; generated images alone are not identity evidence.

## QA

Inspect original/output together at full-product and detail scales. Record specific pass/fail/uncertain observations for geometry (silhouette, proportions, count, arrangement), appearance (texture, colour, transparency), physics (contact, suspension, illumination), and commercial usefulness (crop, clarity, distinct purpose).

Attractive geometry changes fail QA. Uncertain components remain uncertain. Log attempt number, exact prompt and output hash. Two revisions maximum per failing slot; do not claim a complete pack below three passing photos. Owner approval is separate from agent QA. Model instructions do not guarantee perfect fidelity.
