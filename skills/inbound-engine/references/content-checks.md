# Content and claim checks

Read when creating or reviewing an inbound asset.

- Can a real buyer identify the problem and understand the takeaway in the first few seconds or lines?
- Does each factual claim have an owned artifact, public source, verified test or clearly labelled founder statement behind it?
- Is the example our own work, permitted client work, or explicitly hypothetical? Do not disguise a mockup as a screenshot.
- Does the piece teach one useful point before asking for business?
- Is the CTA one concrete action with a working destination?
- Is the brand identity and domain correct at publication time?
- Are customer data, private conversations, contact records, tokens and employer material absent?
- For social: check visual legibility, crop, alt text when available, sound-off comprehension for video, and permission for images and audio.
- For SEO: answer a real question, avoid mass location pages and unsupported ranking claims, and link only where useful.
- For a speed claim: distinguish elapsed time, hands-on work and pre-existing material; cite a trustworthy scope and timestamps or remove the number.

Output a corrected draft plus a short list of any claim that still cannot be verified.
