# Inbound Engine

Turn one observed customer problem or piece of completed work into one useful, verifiable inbound experiment. A good result gives a specific audience an answer and an honest route to ask for help. Do not assume a post, article or automation creates demand.

## Establish the facts

Read the user's brief, current public site and any supplied source artifacts. Separate observed facts, founder statements, hypotheses and unknowns. Select one audience, one problem, one credible takeaway and one next action. If the work involves customer material, use it only with rights to publish; otherwise use a generic or the business's own example. Never fabricate testimonials, time savings, rankings, enquiry counts or revenue.

For Websauce-specific runs, read the current private operating rules and channel ownership **only when available in the authorized workspace**. This public skill does not contain those records or supply authorization to access them.

## Make the experiment

Produce a short content brief, one finished draft in the requested format, a source/evidence note, an exact CTA and a simple distribution plan. Prefer a real project insight over a generic AI tip. Before a live CTA, check that the destination exists and that the enquiry route works using an appropriate, non-sensitive test. If access is missing, state the unverified step and use a CTA that actually works.

Use [content and claim checks](references/content-checks.md) when drafting or reviewing. Use [publication and measurement](references/release-and-learn.md) when a post is due, was attempted, or has results to interpret.

## Act and learn

Save the draft and its evidence in the user's chosen workspace. Publishing, sending or editing a live site depends on the user's authorization and the available account tools; this skill does not grant it. Immediately before publication, verify the destination account, asset, caption, rights, current link, timing and recent posts. Register intent in any existing ledger. Submit once, then record a provider receipt or mark the outcome uncertain and reconcile before retrying.

After publication, record the actual URL and time. Review available reach, clicks, enquiries and qualified conversations on a sensible later date. Missing metrics stay missing. If the result suggests a change, state the evidence and change one thing in the next experiment. Avoid content volume as a substitute for useful work.

For a repeatable daily run, use [the daily template](../../docs/daily-run.md). A sample schedule is a template, not proof that a scheduler is installed or running.
