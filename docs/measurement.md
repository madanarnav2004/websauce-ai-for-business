# What the inbound loop measures

Start with **qualified conversations**: relevant businesses with a concrete problem who chose to contact you. Record the question they asked and where they found you when they tell you. An enquiry is not automatically qualified, and a proposal is not cash collected.

For each asset retain: title/topic, source/evidence, channel, CTA, publication URL and timestamp, available reach/engagement, attributable visits or enquiries, qualified conversations and confirmed revenue. Mark attribution as unknown when the customer did not say or no trustworthy tracking exists. Do not force a click into a sale.

Review weekly. Ask: Which topic caused a useful conversation? Which page or post was saved or shared? Did the enquiry route work? What did buyers misunderstand? Choose the next piece from that evidence. A few posts do not establish an optimal posting hour or a conversion rate.
