# Publication protocol

1. Choose a topic from observed work or a real question. Record the evidence and what is inferred.
2. Draft the asset with a clear CTA. Check the destination and the full enquiry path.
3. Verify the authorized business account and recent posts. Review rights, claims, spelling and visual crop.
4. Before an external submit, record a durable intent. Submit once.
5. Read back the live post/article URL. If uncertain, inspect the destination before any retry.
6. Keep a source of truth for what was actually published and when. Measure downstream enquiries later.

For Websauce, the private operator has a local SQLite receipt ledger. This public repository does not include or connect to it. A reader can use their own ledger; the important invariant is that a draft or scheduled prompt must not be reported as a published result.
