# Choose your first AI task

You do not need to automate your whole business. Pick one task you repeat, whose output you can judge in ten minutes.

A good first task is **frequent**, **text or information heavy**, and **easy to check**. Examples: turning meeting notes into action items, drafting a reply to a common customer question, summarising a product list, or preparing an FAQ from information you already know.

Avoid starting with a task that decides hiring, credit, legal rights, medical outcomes or a large payment. Keep personal and customer records out of your first practice run. Use a fictional or fully redacted example.

## Try one task

1. Write the job in one sentence: “When [trigger] happens, I need [output] so that [person] can [next action].”
2. Gather one safe example and the facts the answer must use.
3. Tell the AI exactly what the output should look like and what it should leave unknown.
4. Compare its output with the real source. Correct errors. Count how long review took.
5. If the result helps, save the prompt and example. Repeat on two more safe cases before automating.

**Copyable starter prompt**

> I run [kind of business]. Help me with one recurring task: [task]. Use only the facts below. Produce [format]. Mark anything missing as “unknown” and do not invent customers, prices or results. Here is a fictional or redacted example: [example]. After the answer, list three things I should verify before using it.

You have a useful workflow when you can describe the input, output and human check. Then decide whether to keep it as a prompt, make a skill, schedule it, or build an app.
