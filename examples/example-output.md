# Example output from the fictional brief

This is a local, unpublished demonstration of the [inbound-engine skill](../skills/inbound-engine/SKILL.md). Cedar Studio is fictional. No account was accessed, no site was tested and no post was published.

## Experiment brief

- Audience: small shop owners who already have a website.
- Problem: a contact form can appear successful without anyone checking where the enquiry lands.
- Evidence: the scenario in [example-brief.md](example-brief.md) is hypothetical. It is not a customer case study.
- Goal: teach one pre-launch check and invite readers to test their own form.
- Format: five-slide carousel.
- Next action: send yourself a test enquiry and confirm the destination.

## Carousel copy

1. **Your form works only when the enquiry arrives.** A success message is the beginning of the check.
2. **Start on a phone.** Open the live contact page the way a customer would.
3. **Send a safe test brief.** Use your own address and label it clearly as a test. Do not submit someone else's personal data.
4. **Check the destination.** Confirm the email, CRM or database record reached the intended place with the details intact.
5. **Test the response path.** Can you find the message and reply? Fix the break before you ask real customers to use the form.

Caption:

> A form can look finished while the enquiry disappears behind it. Before you call your website live, send a test from your phone and check where it actually arrives. A clear success message matters; a retrievable enquiry matters more. Save this for your next launch check.

No link CTA appears because example.org is a reserved fixture, not a working Cedar Studio website.

## Enquiry path check

For a real business, inspect the live page, submit a clearly labelled safe test through its normal supported flow, observe the success state, and verify the exact destination record. Record the outcome and remove or tag test data according to the site's normal policy. If authentication or bot protection blocks the test, report the gap instead of bypassing it.

The fictional fixture provides no real page or inbox, so these steps remain **unverified** in this demo.

## Measurement

If later published by an authorized account, record the actual post URL and time. Review saves/shares, attributable visits, genuine enquiries and qualified conversations after enough time has passed. A draft is not a published post; engagement is not revenue. Choose the next topic from actual audience questions.
